    07Questions

    Before you call.

    Q1Do you build houses or apartments?
    Both. We are house construction and renovation specialists, and our posts include apartment builds.
    Q2Do you do renovations?
    Yes. Renovation is part of what we do, together with house construction. Tell us about your house in the quote helper, or call +63 928 452 6494.
    Q3Can you design the house too?
    Yes. We offer modern, functional home designs, and our Facebook page lists us as Contractor and Architectural Designer.
    Q4Do you build outside Bauan?
    One of our posts asked: DO YOU PLAN TO BUILD AN APARTMENT ANYWHERE IN BATANGAS? For your own lot and town, call or email us.
    Q5How much will my house cost?
    Every lot and design is different, so there are no prices on this page. Send a quote request, or call or email us: jmmpbatsconstruction@gmail.com.
    Q6How long does a build take?
    It depends on the design, the size and the lot. Ask us: call +63 928 452 6494 or email.
    Q7Where are you based?
    Acacia St, Mangoville Subd, Manghinao Proper, Bauan 4201, Batangas. See the map below.
